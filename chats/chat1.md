Built **Loop**, an original bikeshare brand, with a 7-screen iOS signup flow laid out on a canvas:

1. **Welcome** — Blue gradient hero with orange accents
2. **Phone number** — Country picker + numpad
3. **Verify code** — 6-digit OTP with resend timer
4. **Profile** — Name + DOB with gradient avatar
5. **Location** — Map illustration with permission rationale
6. **Payment** — Plan picker (PAYG / Monthly) + Apple Pay
7. **All set** — Confirmation with $5 first-ride credit
